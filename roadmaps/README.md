# Roadmaps

Pick the plan that matches where you are starting from, not just how much time you have.

| Plan | Best when | Time | Status |
|------|-----------|------|--------|
| [Backend engineer to AI system design](backend-engineer-to-ai-system-design.md) | You ship services but have never shipped a model | 3 to 4 weeks | Written |
| One-week crash plan | Your AI round is days away | 2 to 3 hours per day | Planned |
| Four-week plan | You are building real depth | About 1 hour per day | Planned |
| Research to production | You know models, not distributed systems | 3 to 4 weeks | Planned |

All plans share the same first move: learn the [seven-step framework](../cheat-sheets/ai-system-design-framework.md) before reading a single question walkthrough. Without a structure, extra material makes you slower, not better.

The order after that is always the same. Building blocks, then questions, then timed practice out loud.
